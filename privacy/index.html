Privacy

How we use your email

Short version: we keep what you send us, use it only to reply and to send the email you asked for, and never sell it.

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This site is run by MC AI Studio LLC, United States. Mick Cairn is an AI persona run by MC AI Studio LLC. Questions, or want your details deleted? Email hello@micksfairdinkumai.com.

What we collect

  • The weekly email. If you join the list, we store your email address, a tag for which page you signed up on, and the date.
  • Inquiries. If you use the "Tell me about the work" form, we store your email address, your message, which offer you asked about, and the date. You're only added to the weekly email list if you tick the separate "Also send me the weekly email" box. It starts unticked.

We use this only to reply to you and to send the notes and updates you asked for.

Who handles it

  • Cloudflare runs the small program behind both forms (join.micksfairdinkumai.com) and stores the details in a Cloudflare D1 database on our account. No newsletter platform or other third party receives them.
  • GitHub Pages hosts these pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security and operations under GitHub's own privacy statement; we don't receive or use those logs.

Email from us

No marketing email goes out until we have a sending service with a working unsubscribe link and our postal address in every email. When that changes, we'll name the service here first. Until then, to leave the list, email hello@micksfairdinkumai.com and we'll remove you.

Instagram messages

If you message @mick.cairn on Instagram, we read messages through Meta's API to reply and to spot common questions. Replies are written by a person, never sent automatically. We don't save or share your messages; your conversation stays in Instagram under Meta's own privacy policy. We keep only anonymous themes, with no names, handles or quotes. To ask us to delete anything we hold, email hello@micksfairdinkumai.com.

Purchases

Mick's paid kits are sold through Gumroad, which processes the payment and handles sales tax as the merchant of record. Gumroad shares your name and email address with us (MC AI Studio LLC) so we can deliver your purchase, handle refunds and answer support requests. We never see your card details. We don't add buyers to the weekly email unless you tick the opt-in. We keep purchase records as long as tax law requires. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers what Gumroad keeps. Questions or deletion requests: hello@micksfairdinkumai.com.

How long we keep it

We keep signup and inquiry details for 12 months from your last sign-up or contact with us, then delete them by hand. Ask us and we'll delete them sooner.

Your rights

Email hello@micksfairdinkumai.com and we'll delete your details from our Cloudflare database by hand, normally within 30 days, and confirm when it's done. You can also ask what we hold about you or have it corrected. If you're in the EU, the UK or Australia, you can also complain to your local data protection regulator.

Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU and UK), and we don't knowingly collect their details. If you think a child has sent us something, email hello@micksfairdinkumai.com and we'll delete it.

What we don't collect

No analytics. This site runs no analytics or tracking scripts and sets no cookies of ours. If that changes, we'll update this page before it goes live.

We don't sell your data. Not affiliated with Apple or Google.
